# Document-grounded discovery

Read [the interview procedure](grilling.md) and [domain modeling](domain-modeling.md), both bundled with [business-discovery](../SKILL.md). Apply them together: read the supplied documents and relevant project records first, then ask the user only for unresolved business decisions. Keep confirmed terms and decisions in the project's existing glossary and decision records. Follow the interview's rounds and shared-understanding confirmation. No separately installed interview or domain-modeling skill is required.
